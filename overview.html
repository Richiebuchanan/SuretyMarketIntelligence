OVERVIEW

A live intelligence feed, built for underwriting.

One continuously-updated system that reads the surety and construction press, runs a targeted sweep across a watchlist of top accounts and a separate watchlist of broker/producer partners, and transcribes industry podcast episodes — scoring everything it finds for underwriting relevance and turning it into a prioritized, filterable, fully-sourced feed built for both desk-level underwriting and portfolio-level leadership.

Refreshes automatically, every day • 299 articles archived across 124 tracked accounts • 28 broker/producer articles across 56 tracked partners • 15 podcast episodes transcribed • Sourced from ENR, Construction Dive, Insurance Business, Insurance Journal, NASBP, SFAA, Reinsurance News, Bloomberg & more
WHAT IT IS

Surety Market Intelligence is an automated research pipeline, not a static report. Every day it searches 13 tracked trade-press feeds and public sources for surety-relevant news — bond claims and defaults, contractor bankruptcies, infrastructure funding, project awards, rating actions — and runs a targeted sweep across a watchlist of 124 top accounts and a separate watchlist of 56 broker/producer partners, feeding a distinct Broker Watch page focused on distribution-relationship risk. It also transcribes and scores every episode of NASBP's Let's Get Surety podcast, folding that perspective into the same feed. Each item is scored 1–10 for underwriting actionability, classified as Contract or Commercial Surety, summarized, and written into a permanent, live-queried archive. The result is a dashboard that stays current without anyone having to maintain it.

HOW IT WORKS, TODAY
